Legal & Privacy

Setcraft Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 · Last Updated: November 10, 2026

1. Overview

Setcraft ("we", "our", or "us") provides a pro-grade fitness, workout tracking, nutrition planning, and biometric intelligence engine. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, how you control it, and how your data is safeguarded when you use our mobile applications and services.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide and improve your fitness experience:

  • Account Information: Your email address, name, authentication credentials, and account settings when you register or sign in (via Email OTP, Sign in with Apple, or Google Sign-In).
  • Fitness & Profile Data: Age, height, weight, target goals, workout preferences, equipment access, dietary preferences, and allergies that you provide during onboarding or in profile settings.
  • Workout & Nutrition Logs: Exercises performed, sets, repetitions, weights, completed workouts, meals logged, and daily water consumption.
  • Customer Support: Messages and optional screenshots you choose to submit when opening a support or privacy ticket.

3. Apple Health & HealthKit Data (Strict Privacy Guarantee)

HealthKit Compliance Notice: Setcraft adheres strictly to Apple's HealthKit guidelines (App Store Review Guideline 5.1.3).

With your explicit permission, Setcraft reads and/or writes certain health data via Apple HealthKit (or Google Health Connect on Android):

  • Read Access: Steps, active calories burned, workout sessions, and sleep records (and optional weight/body fat if enabled by you).
  • Write Access: Completed workout sessions and estimated active calories logged within Setcraft.

Our Uncompromising Commitments regarding Apple Health data:

  • We NEVER use HealthKit data for marketing, advertising, profiling, or cross-app tracking.
  • We NEVER sell or disclose HealthKit data to advertising platforms, data brokers, or third-party resellers.
  • HealthKit data is NEVER shared with third-party Artificial Intelligence (AI) model providers.
  • You can revoke Setcraft's HealthKit permissions at any time directly in the Apple Health app (under Sharing → Apps & Services → Setcraft).

4. Artificial Intelligence & Plan Generation

Setcraft offers personalized AI-assisted training and nutrition suggestions. Before any data is sent for AI plan generation, the app shows you what will be shared and with whom, and asks you to agree. Nothing is sent if you choose "Not now".

  • Who receives it: AI plan generation is processed by OpenAI, a third-party AI service provider, through its API. If we change provider, the app will ask for your consent again.
  • What is shared with the AI provider: Only your high-level training preferences, entered goals, equipment, food preferences, and recent workout/meal completion logs.
  • What is strictly excluded: Photos, raw Apple HealthKit biometric streams, and sensitive identity data are strictly excluded from AI plan requests.
  • No Public Model Training: Your personal data is sent solely to compute immediate recommendations and is never used to train public foundation models.
  • You can revoke AI planning consent at any time in Settings → Privacy & Data → AI sharing.

4a. Connecting Setcraft to AI Assistants (MCP)

You can optionally connect your Setcraft account to an AI assistant you use, such as Claude by Anthropic, using the Model Context Protocol (MCP). This is off unless you create an access token in Settings → Privacy & Data → MCP.

  • You choose what is shared: each token is read-only and limited to the permissions you select (training profile, weekly workouts, workout history, nutrition, and manually entered body measurements).
  • Never shared through MCP: photos, Apple Health / Health Connect data, email address, password, and billing details.
  • Third party: data requested by the assistant is sent to that assistant's provider (for example Anthropic) and is handled under that provider's privacy policy.
  • Control: tokens expire after 7, 30 or 90 days, and you can revoke them at any time. We keep a 30-day log of token use (tool name, outcome and time, not the data returned), which is included in your data export.

5. Photos & Camera Access

If you choose to use optional body progress features or attach a screenshot to a support ticket, Setcraft requests camera or photo library access:

  • Camera and photo access are strictly optional and only used when you initiate photo capture or selection.
  • Your photos remain private to your account and are never shared publicly or used for model training.
  • You can delete your saved photos at any time in the app.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full ownership and control of your data:

  • Data Export: You can export a full copy of your account data in standard JSON format at any time directly in Settings → Privacy & Data → Export my data.
  • Permanent Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated personal records at any time directly in Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete account.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, workout logs, nutrition history, support tickets, MCP access tokens, and photos are permanently purged from our active databases. If you signed in with Apple, the app asks you to confirm with Apple and your Sign in with Apple authorization is revoked; your linked sign-in identity is also removed from our authentication provider.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Manage it in your store account settings.

7. Security & Third-Party Service Providers

We implement industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking. We use the following service providers only to run Setcraft:

  • Supabase – database and file hosting.
  • Cloudflare – network security and content delivery.
  • Google Firebase – Sign in with Apple / Google Sign-In authentication and push notifications.
  • RevenueCat, Apple App Store and Google Play – subscription purchases and status (we never receive your card details).
  • OpenAI – AI plan generation, only with your consent (see section 4).
  • Anthropic or another AI assistant you connect – only data you allow through an MCP token (see section 4a).

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact our privacy officer:

Email: privacy@setcraft.fit or support@setcraft.fit

You may also submit a privacy inquiry directly within the Setcraft app under Settings → Privacy & Data → Privacy question.
